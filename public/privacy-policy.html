This policy explains how [Business legal name] ("Namma Kadai", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Namma Kadai app or website.

Information we handle

  • Customer orders: name, delivery address, selected items, order status, ratings, and an order identifier.
  • Location: approximate or precise device location when you choose to use nearby-store or delivery-location features. Delivery partners' location is shared only after they explicitly start live location sharing and is used for nearby assignment and order tracking.
  • Store and partner accounts: store details and location; delivery partner name and phone number. Account PINs are stored as password hashes by the server.
  • Payment status: online payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive payment verification details and identifiers, not your full card number or banking password.
  • Preferences: favorites and order IDs may be stored locally on your device.

How we use information

We use this information to show local stores, calculate delivery fees, create and fulfil orders, assign and track deliveries, process ratings, administer store/partner accounts, and verify payments.

Sharing and service providers

We share the information needed to fulfil an order with its store and assigned delivery partner. Razorpay processes online payments under its own terms and privacy policy. The app may open Google Maps for directions and uses Google Fonts to display text. The app backend is hosted by our configured hosting provider. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and retention

Order, store, and delivery data is stored by the backend for operating the service and handling business/legal records. Favorites and saved order identifiers remain in your device's browser/app storage until you clear that storage or uninstall the app. Contact us below to request access, correction, or deletion; requests may be subject to legal and record-keeping requirements.

Security and children's privacy

We use reasonable safeguards, including server-side PIN hashing and HTTPS for production connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Namma Kadai is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13.
